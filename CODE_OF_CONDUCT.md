# Code of conduct

Proof@home adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in this repository, on proofathome.org, and in every space the project runs, including the leaderboard, team pages and project channels.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, accept that people differ, and keep criticism about the work. Harassment, insults, personal attacks and publishing someone's private information are not tolerated.

The project also has a rule of its own: **do not try to game the program.** Submitting results meant to slip past the checker, farming points with duplicate accounts, or hoarding claims is a conduct issue as well as a technical one. If you find a way the checker could be fooled, report it as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Reporting

Email **conduct@proofathome.org**. Reports are read only by the maintainers who handle conduct and are kept confidential. If a maintainer is involved, say so in the email and they will not take part in handling it.

We aim to reply within a week. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's guidelines: a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, depending on the impact.
